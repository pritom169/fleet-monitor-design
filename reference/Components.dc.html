11

Pins and map controls. Problems stand up. The rest stays flat.

A problem robot is a pin with a glyph and a short tail to its exact spot. A healthy robot is a 9 px dot. A pin keeps its size on screen when you zoom, because its scale is the inverse of the map zoom. A selected robot gets a blue ring that breathes.
